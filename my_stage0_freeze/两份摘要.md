# 框架 A：应用优先（当前定位）

目标场子：IEEE TWC / TVT / ICC / GLOBECOM 一类通信场子。

## A-GO

> **Graph Neural Network Based Branching for UAV Emergency Network Deployment**
>
> Rapid deployment of UAV-mounted base stations after a disaster requires jointly optimizing placement, user association, and downlink bandwidth allocation subject to backhaul capacity, per-UAV energy budgets, and minimum service guarantees for mission-critical terminals. We formulate this as a mixed-integer linear program with a normalized priority-weighted objective, and accelerate its solution by replacing the branching-variable selection rule of an exact solver with a graph convolutional network trained by imitation of full strong branching. The learned policy scores only the solver's legal branching candidates at each node, so the feasible region and all optimality certificates are preserved, and any failure falls back to the default rule. On ⟨120⟩ in-distribution instances the learned rule improves the normalized primal-dual integral by ⟨X⟩% over default SCIP branching, with ⟨Y⟩% median wall-clock speedup on jointly solved instances; end-to-end timing includes graph extraction, inference, and post-processing. We further evaluate on three out-of-distribution families that vary instance size, scenario geometry, and backhaul conditions, and report ⟨Z⟩ tail slowdown at P95.

**评价**：能发，是一篇干净的应用论文。贡献 = 领域实例 + 实现严谨性。**但它的 claim 本质上是"Gasse 的方法在 UAV 场景也能用"。**

## A-STOP

> **On the Limited Benefit of Learned Branching for UAV Deployment MILPs**
>
> We formulate post-disaster UAV network deployment as a MILP and investigate whether GNN-based branching improves solution time. We find that default SCIP branching already solves ⟨X⟩% of instances within ⟨2⟩ seconds and that full strong branching reduces the search tree by only ⟨Y⟩%, leaving insufficient room for a learned policy. We therefore do not observe speedup.

**评价**：**这不是一篇论文。** 它读起来像"我们试了，没成"。三个致命问题：

1. 没有可复用的方法贡献——只有一个具体问题上的一次观测；
2. 审稿人的第一反应是"你的实例太小/建模有问题"，而你没有任何东西可以反驳；
3. 全部前期投入（七道门、契约、审计）在摘要里完全不可见。

**这就是当前框架的风险敞口。**

# 框架 B：协议优先（建议）

目标场子（需与导师确认）：Mathematical Programming Computation、INFORMS Journal on Computing、CPAIOR、NeurIPS Datasets & Benchmarks 一类重视计算严谨性与可复现性的场子。通信场子仍可作为第二篇的落点。

## B-STOP　★ 这份是关键

> **When Does Learning to Branch Not Pay Off? A Pre-Registered Protocol and a Negative Result on UAV Network Deployment**
>
> Work on learning to branch reports speedups over default solver heuristics across a growing set of MILP families. The complementary evidence — problem classes for which learning does *not* pay off — is largely absent from the literature, and there is no standard way to find out before paying the cost of expert-label generation and training. We propose a pre-registered protocol that answers this question *before* a single training label is produced. The protocol freezes seven quantitative stop/go gates: four opportunity and economics gates covering non-degenerate instance difficulty, availability of decision nodes in the early search tree, the structural headroom of a perfect expert (full strong branching) relative to the default rule, and whether measured end-to-end inference overhead can be amortized by that headroom; and three correctness gates covering transformed-LP candidate mapping, expert-interface parity, and label completeness. All thresholds, the analysis code, and the decision rule are frozen and hashed before execution.
>
> We execute the protocol on a post-disaster UAV network deployment MILP that jointly optimizes base-station placement, user association, and downlink bandwidth allocation under backhaul, gateway, and per-UAV energy constraints. Crucially, we first strengthen the formulation with edge-specific bounds and a valid inequality that leave the integer hull unchanged, so that measured headroom is not an artifact of a loose big-M. Escalating along a pre-declared size ladder from ⟨60⟩ to ⟨150⟩ users, full strong branching reduces the search tree by a median of only ⟨X⟩% at every rung, against a frozen threshold of 20%. The opportunity gate fails, and we report `STOP` rather than proceeding to training.
>
> The protocol consumed ⟨10⟩ CPU hours and avoided an estimated ⟨130⟩ CPU hours of label generation, training, and evaluation that would have produced no speedup. We additionally report an incidental finding: a static pre-implementation audit falsified our own design assumption that per-UAV energy budgets would be an active constraint, showing them to be statically redundant across the entire instance family. We release the generator, the strengthened formulation, the gate implementation, and all negative-result logs.

**为什么这是一篇论文**：

| 贡献 | 是否依赖 GNN 成功 |
|---|---|
| 可复用的预注册协议（七道门 + 判定规则） | 否 |
| 一个明确问题类的 headroom 负结果，附规模阶梯 | 否 |
| "早期检测的成本 vs 避免的浪费"这个比值 | 否 |
| 诚实强化 vs 松 Big-M 对 headroom 测量的影响 | 否 |
| 静态审计推翻自身假设的实例 | 否 |
| 完整 artifact | 否 |

**四条都不依赖 GNN 跑赢。**

## B-GO

> **A Pre-Registered Protocol for Learning to Branch, with a Case Study on UAV Network Deployment**
>
> ⟨与 B-STOP 相同的第一段：协议动机与七道门⟩
>
> We execute the protocol on a post-disaster UAV network deployment MILP. All seven gates pass: full strong branching reduces the search tree by a median of ⟨X⟩%, and shadow inference with the final architecture costs ⟨Y⟩% of default solve time. We therefore proceed to label generation and training, and evaluate a GNN branching policy that scores only the solver's legal candidates, preserving the feasible region and all optimality certificates. On ⟨120⟩ in-distribution and ⟨180⟩ out-of-distribution instances spanning size, geometry, and backhaul shifts, the learned rule improves the normalized primal-dual integral by ⟨Z⟩%, with ⟨W⟩ P95 tail slowdown; all timing is end-to-end.
>
> Beyond the speedup itself, the pre-registered design lets us ask a question the literature has not answered cleanly: **does measured expert headroom predict realized learned speedup?** We pre-declared an expected net upside of ⟨E⟩ from the gate measurements alone, and realized ⟨R⟩, an imitation capture rate of ⟨C⟩. We discuss what this ratio implies for deciding, in advance, whether a new MILP family is worth the cost of learning to branch.

**注意最后一段。** 它把 B1 的 `expected_net_upside` 从内部质检字段升级成了一个科学问题——**"专家 headroom 能不能预测学习方法的实际收益"**。这个问题的答案对整个子领域有用，而且只有预注册设计才能干净地回答。

**所以框架 B 不只是 STOP 时的保险，它在 GO 时也比框架 A 更有贡献。**

# 结论：这个练习问出了什么

## 1. 反转框架

**A-STOP 不是论文，B-STOP 是。** 而 B-GO 严格优于 A-GO，因为它多一个 headroom 预测性的发现。

框架 B 在两种结局下都占优，**现在改的成本是重写标题和引言，第六个月改的成本是重做实验设计**。

## 2. 一个之前没意识到的依赖关系

写 B-STOP 的时候暴露出一件事：

> **单个规模上的一次负结果不是论文。**
>
> "我们的 60 用户实例上 full strong 只压了 8%" —— 审稿人会说"实例太小"，然后拒掉。
> "我们沿预声明阶梯爬到 150 用户，每一级 headroom 都低于 ⟨X⟩%" —— 这是发现。

所以 **C1 的规模阶梯不是备选方案，是 STOP 论文能否存在的前置条件。** 它的优先级应该从"失败后再说"提到"现在就定，而且要和导师确认阶梯的现实性辩护"。

这也改变了侦察 spike 的设计：如果侦察给出弱信号，**不要停下来**，而是直接按阶梯往上爬一级再测——因为那一级的数据无论如何都要进论文。

## 3. 摘要里必须保留的一句话

B-STOP 第二段那句"we first strengthen the formulation ... so that measured headroom is not an artifact of a loose big-M"。

这句话是整篇 STOP 论文的防线：它预先挡掉"你的问题不难是因为你建模建松了"这个最致命的审稿意见。**没有这句话，负结果论文站不住。**
